Vision Test

Screening estimate only — not a prescription

⚠️ This is a screening estimate, not a clinical eye examination. Because screen size, display settings and viewing distance can affect results, this test cannot determine your exact spectacle power or replace an examination by an optometrist or ophthalmologist.

Before you start

Follow these steps so the screening is as reliable as possible.

  1. Sit about 40 cm (arm's length) away from your screen.
  2. Set your screen brightness to a comfortable, normal level.
  3. Make sure the room is well lit with no glare on the screen.
  4. If you normally wear glasses or lenses, keep them on.
  5. Cover one eye with your palm when testing that eye separately.
