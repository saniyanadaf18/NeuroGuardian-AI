Why can headaches happen?

Headaches can have several possible triggers. Your answers help the assistant identify patterns.

🧠 Migraine pattern

Migraine can involve moderate to severe headache, sometimes on one side, with nausea or sensitivity to light and sound.

😣 Tension-type pattern

A pressure-like or tight feeling around the forehead or both sides can occur with tension-type headaches.

💧 Dehydration

Not drinking enough fluids may contribute to headache symptoms in some people.

😴 Sleep and stress

Poor sleep, stress and prolonged work or study may contribute to headache symptoms.

👁️ Eye strain

Long screen exposure and visual strain can be associated with headache and eye discomfort.
